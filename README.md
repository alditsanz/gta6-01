An original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for the
[make.fun gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).

**Solmar Heat** — steal, drive, run, survive. Explore the sun-soaked city
of Solmar on foot and by car: a 9×9 street grid across 10 districts
(Sunset Mile, Cactus Court, Neon Strip, Marina, Pier 9, Canal Row and
more), traffic and pedestrians, 12 stealable cars, a 5-star wanted level
with SCPD pursuit, roadblocks and a helicopter, and a 7-job story with
Lena Voss and Kane that ends in "The Last Ledger" heist at SCPD
Headquarters.

Side work: taxi fares, street races, package deliveries, turf wars,
10 hidden Solmar coins, near-miss bonuses, and Lou's Paint Shop — which
now repaints your car as well as clearing heat and repairing it. A
day/night cycle, weather, and rush-hour traffic (twice the cars at peak
hours, half at night) keep the city alive. Progress saves automatically.

## Play

Serve `game/` with any static file server and open `game/index.html` in
desktop Chrome. No network requests — everything is local files.

- WASD / arrows — move & drive
- E — steal / exit car
- Mouse — aim & shoot, R reload, F punch
- Space — handbrake, Shift — sprint
- T — taxi fare, L — delivery at Harbor Market, G — turf war
- C — repaint at Lou's Paint Shop, P — pause, M — mute

A wholly original game. No part of it comes from any commercial game.
